<objective>
Apply the owner's five post-Phase-26 UAT formatting rules (enumerated verbatim on flox, 2026-06-10) to BOTH the design law and the Phase-26 rebuilt adjustment screens. This is a layout/sizing-only conformance pass — it must NOT change icon GLYPH choices, command/clamp/busy-lock/build-once semantics, or any printer behavior.

The five rules:
1. **Icons too small** — prominent icons should be big and vibrant, ~70-80% of U. EXCEPTION: detailed/dense list panes keep their (smaller) icon size.
2. **Space between icon · name · value** — name start-aligned with the icon, measurement/value end-aligned, with breathing room between.
3. **Scrubber ≤1U height** — the current-style scrubber track+thumb is limited to a single U.
4. **Focus top-left e-stop reserve** — keep useful content out of the top-left of the Focus (or the list field in single view); reserved for the floating emergency stop.
5. **Controls ≤1U height** — most controls follow the 1U height limit unless specifically chosen otherwise; no Output control needs to be bigger than 1U EXCEPT the LED color wheel.

Purpose: lock these owner decisions into the law so future UI work auto-conforms, and bring the just-rebuilt Phase-26 screens into conformance now.
Output: updated LAYOUT.md / COMPONENTS.md / SKILL.md + sizing/alignment/height edits across the six adjustment components/screens.
</objective>
