(A) printing/paused → existing `StatGrid` UNCHANGED; (B) not-printing + a last job exists →

and stub the two future tap-through surfaces (B → past-print detail, C → file browser) as one-line nav
wiring points without building the destinations.

AppContainer/MoonrakerService plumbing, the canonical doc capture, and the state-driven field lambda.
