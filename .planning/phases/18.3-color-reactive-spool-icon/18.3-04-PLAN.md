<objective>
Wire `SpoolGlyph` into all five agreed surfaces (D-06.1–.5) with the D-07/D-08 color precedence, then
verify legibility on-device (flox, real Adreno 320). This is the payoff wave: every spool-icon
surface becomes color-reactive with the universal empty-spool fallback.

Two structural realities the revision pins down (verified against source):
1. The App Drawer's `DRAWER_TILES` is a file-level static `private val` of `DrawerTileSpec`s rendered
   from a `symbol: String` — a closing-over `glyph` lambda baked into the static spec CANNOT read the
   per-call `spoolSwatches`. The reactive Spool tile is therefore a SPECIAL-CASE inside the `DrawerTile`
   composable (branch on `tile.dest == Dest.Spool`), NOT a lambda on the static spec.
2. No always-live active-spool DETAIL (color-bearing) flow exists today: `AppShell` collects only
   `activeSpoolStatus` (status + id), and `SpoolHolder` carries `activeStatus: SpoolmanStatus?` plus a
   `spools` list that only populates after `SpoolScreen` calls `holder.load()`. The drawer tile needs a
   dedicated `activeSpoolDetail` flow that fetches + keeps the active spool's full `SpoolmanSpool` by id.

Owner decision (D-07 reconciliation): ALL spool surfaces stay Spoolman-gated exactly as today. No
un-gating. No-Spoolman users see NO spool surface (unchanged UX). The gcode `filament_colors[0]` tier
fires ONLY on the PrintStatus surfaces and ONLY when Spoolman IS present but the active record has a
null/missing color. The App Drawer tile uses Spoolman-color → empty spool only (no gcode middle tier).

Purpose: Deliver SC2 (renders tinted by actual filament color on the agreed surfaces), SC4 (sensible
empty-spool fallback everywhere), and SC5 (legible across themes — verified on the hardware floor).
Output: a live `activeSpoolDetail` flow; reactive spool on the launcher tile, mid-print shortcut,
drawer tile, and both SpoolScreen surfaces; the on-device UAT sign-off.
</objective>
