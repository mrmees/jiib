rendering the same printer-screen layout, both driven by one byte-identical in-process 2-4 Hz

`FrameTimingMetric` as corroboration only.

by 01-01), a Macrobenchmark/UiAutomator driver, and the Python framestats parser. NO on-device

the connection layer.
