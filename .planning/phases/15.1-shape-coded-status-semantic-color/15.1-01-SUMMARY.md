# Phase 15.1 Plan 01: Wave-0 Foundation Summary

Wave-0 foundation for shape-coded status + semantic color: two distinct shape-glyph drawable
assets, three compile-clean RED test scaffolds, a strict-JSON OKLCH ramp golden fixture with its
independent reproducibility oracle, and the locked `ThemeTuple` status-override typing decision that
unblocks every later wiring plan.

## What Was Built

### Task 1 — Type decision + shape-glyph assets (commit `9da3058`)
- `ic_status_octagon.xml` — a regular 8-gon OUTER contour (8 vertices at 45° spacing, radius 42 about
  centre 48,48, flat-top STOP-sign orientation) plus an inner `✕` mark. White stroke only, tinted at
  the call site (`t.stop`) via the `nozzle.xml` convention.
- `ic_status_triangle.xml` — an upward 3-gon OUTER contour (apex top, base across bottom) plus an
  inner `!` (bar + dot). White stroke only, tinted at the call site (`t.heat` = caution).
- **D-01 honored:** the two shapes differ at the OUTER SILHOUETTE (8-gon vs 3-gon), not just the inner
  mark, so the silhouette IS the safety mechanism (survives grayscale/CVD). **D-02 honored:** only
  these two shapes; NO circle for `go`. No Unicode glyphs (those are HTML-preview-only). No status hex
  baked in (THEME-01).
- Both keep the canonical 96dp authoring box; consumers (plans 05/06/07) MUST set explicit fsSp-scaled
  bounds — a raw 96dp compound drawable would blow up a console row (Item 6). Recorded for downstream.
- `:app:processDebugResources` — BUILD SUCCESSFUL (assets parse as valid `<vector>`).

### Task 2 — RED scaffolds + golden fixture + oracle (commit `17e02dd`)
- `SeriesColorTest` (D-05 N-series): 3 modes × wrap + empty-pool guard — `fail(...)` bodies, future
  symbols named in PROSE only.

## Wave-0 Type Decision (LOCKED — consumed by plans 02 & 04)

Per RESEARCH Open-Question-1: the `ThemeTuple` gains a **SEPARATE `statusOverrides: Map<String,Long>`
field**. The existing Int-keyed `poolOverrides: Map<Int,Long>` cannot hold the string status keys
`"stop"`/`"caution"`/`"go"`. ONE persisted DataStore wire map (already `String→Long` in
`Profile.poolOverrides`) holds both pool indices and status keys; the split into the typed
`statusOverrides` happens at the tuple/`TokenBridge` boundary. No code change to `ThemePrefs` in this
plan — only the recorded decision + the assets/scaffolds. Plan 02 (TokenBridge split) and plan 04
(persistence + fail-safe sanitize) depend on this.

## Ramp Parameterization (LOCKED — plan 03 implements TO this)

32 stops, index 0→31, `endpointsInclusive = true`. Three OKLCH control points:
low `oklch(0.45 0.12 255)` → mid `oklch(0.65 0.13 150)` → high `oklch(0.85 0.15 95)`. Piecewise-linear
across two segments split at the mid control point anchored at **stop index 15** (segment A = stops
0..15 low→mid; segment B = stops 15..31 mid→high). Hue along the shorter arc per segment; L and C
linear. Lowercase `#rrggbb`, no alpha. Plan 03's Kotlin baker asserts bit-for-bit against `ramp[]`
and may NOT regenerate it from Kotlin output.
