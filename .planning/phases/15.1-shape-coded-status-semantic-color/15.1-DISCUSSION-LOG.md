## Pool→role semantic map

| Question | Options | Selected |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| Top-3 dual-tagging | Accept (data shares directional colors) · Separate | **Accept dual-tagging** |
| maxItems / N-series | (owner reframed — see below) | **No cap; wrap; mode-collapse model** |
| Canonical sets accent-vs-pool | Pin to pool, accent generic-only · Accent always leads | **Accent always leads** |

**User's choice & notes:** Owner reframed the maxItems question into the N-series collapse model:
three color sources (accent / text / pool) combine and collapse onto duplicates in Simple & HC;
accent + pool carry arbitrarily large sets in Colorful; no arbitrary cap, just wrap/repeat.
Worked example (5-line graph): Colorful = accent,pool0,pool1,pool2,pool3 · Simple = accent,text
alternating · HC = accent,stop,caution,go,text. Claude affirmed this is cleaner than the spec on two
points (Colorful never touches status colors; HC safely reuses RYG because status carries shape) and
flagged the one conflict: the temp graph is a *canonical* set (nozzle=pool[0] locked in Phase-15 D-2)
where trace must match readout. Owner chose **accent always leads, even canonical sets** (nozzle =
accent, bed = pool[0], chamber = pool[1]) — rationale: keep the user's accent prominent on every
screen, since the whitespace-heavy minimal motif washes out personalization. **This supersedes
Phase-15 D-2/D-13** and reconceives directional (temperature→accent, xy→pool[0], z→pool[1]).
