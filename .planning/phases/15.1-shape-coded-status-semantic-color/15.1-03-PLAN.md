<objective>
Replace the bed-mesh view's `accent→surface2→stop` sRGB lerp with a dedicated perceptually-uniform
OKLCH sequential ramp kept OFF pure red/green (D-11), so a tall spot reads as "tall," not "FAILED."
The OKLCH math already exists in `Palette.kt` — this plan adds interpolation + stop selection only.

Purpose: Sequential height data is its own sub-system, distinct from the categorical pool. A red/green
ramp falsely implies pass/fail on a mesh.
Output: `OklchRamp.kt`, a rewired `BedMeshHeatmapView`, and a GREEN golden test.
</objective>
