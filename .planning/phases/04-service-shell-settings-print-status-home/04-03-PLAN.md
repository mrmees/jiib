<objective>
The first real app wiring: an `Application` class, a process-scoped service-locator
(`AppContainer`), and the **foreground service** that owns the Moonraker spine across
Activity recreation and screen-off — replacing the static DevConfig with the persisted
connection (04-01), driven by the pure primitives (04-02).

Purpose: SHELL-03 + CONN-01 — the service is the lifecycle owner that makes the connection
durable. This is the structural heart of the phase.

This revision nails down three cross-plan seams the review flagged:
- **SessionControl (concern #1):** a narrow interface (`requestReconnectNow()`,
  `restartFirmware()`, `restartHost()`) that 04-05 (Splash) and 04-06 (Stop) consume — the raw
  `MoonrakerSession` is NEVER leaked to the UI.
- **Atomic spine publication (concern #6):** all spine references live in ONE immutable
  `SpineHandle` published via a single `StateFlow<SpineHandle?>`; a rebuild swaps the whole
  handle atomically so partially-swapped flow/session refs are unrepresentable.
- **Rotation continuity (concern #3):** the handle carries a monotonic `sessionInstanceId`
  (and exposes the spine instance identity) so the rotation gate asserts CONTINUITY of the same
  instance, not the absence of a transition.

Output: `DinghyApp`, `AppContainer`, `SpineHandle`, `SessionControl`, `MoonrakerService`,
`ServiceNotification`, manifest wiring.
</objective>
