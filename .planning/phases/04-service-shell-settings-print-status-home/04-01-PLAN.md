<objective>
Persist the user's Moonraker connection (host/port/optional key) so it survives app restarts
and replaces the static `DevConfig`, plus a best-effort mDNS discovery helper for Settings.

Purpose: CONN-01 — the connection must be user-editable and durable. This is the data layer
the Settings screen (04-04) writes and the service (04-03) reads to construct the spine.
Output: `ConnectionConfig` model, `ConnectionStore` (DataStore), `MdnsDiscovery` helper.

mDNS is locked IN-SCOPE by CONTEXT D-04 (additive scan in Settings, manual entry is the floor).
Per review concern #5, the discovery machinery is made FULLY LAZY: constructing `MdnsDiscovery`
acquires no system service and registers no listener; all `NsdManager` work happens only when
`discover()` is collected (the user taps Scan in 04-04). The multicast lock (if needed) is also
acquired only inside the flow and released on cancellation. No auto-connect from discovery.
</objective>
