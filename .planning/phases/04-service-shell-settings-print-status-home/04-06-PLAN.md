<objective>
Build the Print Status home (SHELL-04) — the monitor/landing surface that proves the Phase-3
render/throttle primitives **in anger** on the Adreno 320: a state-adaptive Focus (printing →
`ProgressRing`; idle → live temp/status readout, D-08), a 2×3 numeric stat grid, and a compact
heater **sparkline** via the Views `GraphView` (D-09). The gutter shows Stop + greyed Tune/Pause
placeholders (D-07); Stop routes through the full-screen `ConfirmGuard` and fires
`printer.emergency_stop` (SHELL-02/D-10).

⚠ PERF WATCH (D-09): ring + sparkline + live numeric grid on one screen is a NEW render surface for
the Adreno 320 — it must be measured against the Phase-3 two-part gate (`03-PERF-RESULTS.md` re-open
conditions). The on-device gfxinfo checkpoint is in this plan.

Purpose: The app finally shows a live printer at a glance and gives the firmware panic halt one
confirmed tap away — and the shared render primitive earns its keep on real hardware.
Output: `PrintStatusHolder` (toolkit-agnostic), `PrintStatusScreen`, a host-side holder test, and an
on-device perf checkpoint.
</objective>
