<objective>
Replace AppShell's ~1000-line `when(dest)` hub-and-spoke holder with a Navigation-Compose `NavHost`
back-stack — the heart of the phase. Hoist all ~20 session holders ABOVE the NavHost (preserve every
`remember(store)` re-key and every `DisposableEffect { onDispose { holder.cancel() } }` leak-cancel
verbatim — there are 4: webcam/spool/console/macro), keep all overlays floating as Box siblings above the
NavHost, HOIST the printing-only `FloatingEStop` + its Stop Confirm guard to the AppShell overlay layer so
it renders on EVERY destination (FIX-1), and convert the swipe-suppress set, the webcam lifecycle effect,
and the AppShell-level BackHandlers to read `navBackStackEntry` instead of the retired `dest` enum. Wire the
D-04 pop-to-root on print start/end via the pure `shouldPopToRoot` predicate (from 24-01). Slim
`ShellNavState` to carry only in-screen sub-nav state.

The four in-screen sub-navs (Calibration routine, Fine-Tune group, Outputs detail, Macros
bookmarked-vs-system) STAY in-screen (D-01) — do NOT promote them to nav routes.

Decisions honored by the gate-above structure: the connection lifecycle stays the TOP tier of the
waterfall (D-02) — `RootController`'s `when{}` and `TopRoute.derive()` are UNCHANGED, the NavHost mounts
only in Shell state, so the not-ready connection states remain HARD OVERRIDES the back-stack cannot
dismiss (D-03), and no new onboarding/wizard is built. The App Drawer stays LIVE as-is (D-12) — it
remains a floating overlay above the NavHost and is the interim System hub; its inert "Power" tile stays
unwired (D-11). None of these change behavior; they are preserved through the migration.

This plan does NOT touch PrintStatusScreen's internals (the morph + idle list is this plan's sibling
plan 24-04) — `composable<NavDest.WaterfallHome>` mounts the existing PrintStatusScreen with its
`onNavigate` seam now typed `(NavDest) -> Unit`. ⚠ FIX-1: the FloatingEStop that 24-04 used to wire INSIDE
PrintStatusScreen now lives HERE at the AppShell overlay layer (so it appears on Move/Extrude/Console/etc.
while printing, per D-14 "every screen only when printing"); 24-04 keeps only the morph/idle-list work and
removes its in-screen e-stop wiring.

⚠ ACCEPTED REGRESSION (owner-locked 2026-06-09, FIX-3): after a reconnect/recovery Splash the user LANDS
ON WaterfallHome (the morphing root) AND each in-screen sub-nav RESETS to its hub. The top-level drill-down
back-stack is NOT preserved across the recovery Splash (the NavHost is composition-local inside AppShell
and decomposes during the Splash, gate-above), and `applyEntryReset` clears the Calibration routine /
FineTune group / Macros sub-nav on the next entry — so in-screen sub-nav does NOT survive either. This is
the deliberate, simpler path the owner accepted (replacing the old dest-preservation gate at
AppShell.kt:854). It is NOT a bug; the executor and verifier must EXPECT both the land-on-root AND the
sub-nav reset.

Purpose: Retire the hand-rolled back-stack for the real Nav-Compose one (predictive-back, process-death
restoration, type-safe routes) without tearing down session state on navigation. Output: a NavHost shell.
</objective>
