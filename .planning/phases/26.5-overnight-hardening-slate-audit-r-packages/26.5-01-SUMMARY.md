**2. [Rule 3 - Blocking] NullSafeMutableLiveData detector disabled**
- **Found during:** Task 2 step 4 (second/third `updateLintBaseline` attempts)
- **Issue:** Lint still crashed with `IncompatibleClassChangeError` — pinned to ONE detector, `androidx.lifecycle.lint.NonNullableMutableLiveDataDetector`. The lint worker's stack frames showed it still ran under the daemon's JDK 21 (the toolchain governs compilation, not the lint worker), and this specific lifecycle-lint/AGP-8.7 mismatch is a known crasher.
- **Fix:** `disable += "NullSafeMutableLiveData"` in the lint block with an explanatory comment. The app contains zero LiveData (StateFlow/Flow everywhere) so the check can never fire here. This is NARROWER and STRONGER than the plan's UAST-CRASH FALLBACK (which would have dropped to CI-only enforcement with `checkReleaseBuilds=false`): local `lintDebug`, `lintVitalRelease`, AND CI all enforce with `abortOnError=true`.
- **Files modified:** app/build.gradle.kts
- **Commit:** `af75fcb`
