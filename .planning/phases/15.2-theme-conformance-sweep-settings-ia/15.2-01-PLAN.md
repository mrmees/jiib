<objective>
Build the transient theme-override LAYER — the engine that lets a single in-memory override flip the
whole live app (both Compose and classic-Views surfaces) without ever persisting, plus the app-global
dev-widget enable boolean and the Wave-0 RED scaffold + AUDIT.md artifact. This is the phase-early
foundation (D-09): both the guided review (D-10) and the app-wide audit (D-11) ride on the cycler,
and the cycler rides on this override layer.

Per the cross-AI review (HIGH-1..3, HIGH-5) and the Codex confirmation pass, the override layer is
hardened: `effectiveTokens` is a PURE function of its combine inputs — it BAKES from the canonical
`activeThemeTuple` in BOTH the persisted and the override branch and NEVER reads the async-lagged
`themeResolver.tokens.value` (HIGH-1, the foundational fix — the resolver's `tokens` is applied
ASYNCHRONOUSLY by `seedTheme()` after `activeThemeTuple` emits, so reading it imperatively in the
non-override branch can emit a stale snapshot and never self-correct under a profile/theme change);
a SINGLE canonical `activeThemeTuple` flow feeds BOTH `seedTheme()` and `effectiveTokens` (no idle-tuple
race, no profile skew); baking is PURE via `ThemeResolver.bake(tuple)` (no shared-mutable-resolver
tearing under multiple collectors); the override carries the FULL ThemeTuple with only the cycled axes
substituted (no dropped fields, notably `statusOverrides`); an active override steps ATOMICALLY via
`updateThemeOverride { transform }` (rapid-tap-safe); and the override is cleared/ignored when the
dev-enable boolean goes false (no stranded override).

Purpose: Make per-screen theme conformance checks cheap — one device walk per combo instead of six
rebuilds — without corrupting the persisted per-profile theme tuple, and without the override layer
racing or diverging from the real theme.
Output: `ThemeOverride.kt`, the pure `ThemeResolver.bake(tuple)`, the `AppContainer` override plumbing
(`activeThemeTuple` + pure `effectiveTokens` + `setThemeOverride`/`updateThemeOverride` + disable-clears),
the `DinghyTheme` collect swap, the app-global dev-enable boolean, host-pure tests, and the generated
AUDIT.md scaffold.
</objective>
