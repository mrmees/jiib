Purpose: media3-exoplayer-rtsp/-hls are the only path that hardware-decodes the printers' H.264
(D-01). Pin **>= 1.10.1** (the latest stable at planning time) — do NOT pin 1.10.0. The load-bearing
reason for staying on a recent 1.10.x is the RTSP fix for H.264 access units that span multiple RTP
packets (exactly the 720p / 1 s-GOP keyframes these cameras emit, RESEARCH Pitfall 4); the executor
must CONFIRM that fix is present in the resolved artifact rather than trusting a specific bug-version
claim (do NOT assert which point release the bug was in — 1.10.1 is the conservative pin). The floor
guard is the project's central trap: a transitive dep could move the merged-manifest minSdk above 23.

Output: a buildable tree with media3 on the classpath, a passing floor guard, and compiling RED
scaffolds that fail until plans 03/04 implement their targets.
