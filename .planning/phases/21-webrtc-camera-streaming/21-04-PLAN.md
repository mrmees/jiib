<objective>
Build the on-device player binding as a COMPOSITE feed that fits the existing ladder: `Media3Feed`
(the H.264-first `WebcamFeed` that builds an ExoPlayer, forces RTSP-over-TCP, attaches a SurfaceView,
and — crucially — on a decoder failure internally FALLS THROUGH to the existing MJPEG/Snapshot probe
path within the SAME `run()`, exactly like `bitmapFeed` already chains MJPEG→Snapshot→Terminal),
`Media3SurfaceHost` (the `AndroidView<SurfaceView>` host mirroring `WebcamViewHost`), and the
`WebcamHolder` wiring that routes H.264-selected cams through it. The lead transport (RTSP or HLS)
and the SurfaceView-cutout chrome follow the recorded spike DECISION (`21-SPIKE-RESULT.md`). Closes
the last two Wave-0 RED scaffolds (Media3FeedOutcome, RtspSourceConfig) and extends
WebcamReconnectStateTest. Deletes the throwaway spike.

Purpose: this is the genuinely-new shipped work — narrow by design (PATTERNS: "almost everything except
build-an-ExoPlayer-force-TCP-attach-a-SurfaceView-map-its-errors is already built"). The two real
hazards are the main-thread ExoPlayer confinement (a DIVERGENCE from the IO-dispatched bitmapFeed) and
the leak-free release (the project's frozen-feed-after-restart history, WR-01). The reconnect/backoff/
dead-end machinery is REUSED verbatim. ⚠ The fall-through is the load-bearing correctness point: the
holder's `drive()` STOPS on `FeedOutcome.Terminal` (→ DeadEnd, `return`) and does NOT try a lower rung
— so the H.264-decoder-verifies-then-falls-to-MJPEG/Snapshot logic (D-10, SC2) MUST live INSIDE the
composite feed's single `run()`, mirroring how `bitmapFeed` internally falls MJPEG→Snapshot before it
ever returns `Terminal`. A standalone Media3Feed that returns `Terminal` on a decoder error would
dead-end the screen instead of falling back — that is the bug this plan must NOT ship.

Output: a wired H.264 rung that renders on-device, falls through to MJPEG/snapshot on decoder error
(in-feed, not via a holder-Terminal), releases cleanly, plus green CAM-12/14/16 unit coverage and a
removed spike.
</objective>
