<objective>
Convert the three provided source brand SVGs (`img/jiib_icon.svg`, `img/jiib_name.svg`,
`img/jiib_name_icon_stack.svg`) into Android VectorDrawables (D-08 — Android cannot render raw
SVG). This is the upstream asset plan: every downstream surface (launcher icon, splash, about)
consumes one of these three drawables, so this plan owns asset creation exclusively and runs in
Wave 1 with no dependencies.

Per D-07, the two in-app marks (wordmark + lockup) are flattened to a SINGLE `android:fillColor`
so a Compose `Icon(tint=…)` recolors the whole drawable uniformly. Per D-03, the launcher
foreground mark is baked FIXED WHITE (`#FFFFFFFF`) — it lives outside the theme token system.

Purpose: produce the tintable/fixed assets the rest of the phase wires up.
Output: three new files under `app/src/main/res/drawable/`.
</objective>
