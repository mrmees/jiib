## Sizing Note (Pitfall 5 — preserved magnitude, render-path caveat)

A vector `painterResource` auto-fills its modifier box (`weight().aspectRatio(1f)` / `fillMaxHeight(0.5f)`), whereas a ligature glyph is a `Text` sized in `sp` and does NOT auto-fill. To avoid a silent shrink, the converted sites use fixed `fsSp` magnitudes consistent with the app's established convention (e.g. `ScrewsTiltScreen` calibration-hero `fsSp(56f)`, jog/button glyphs `fsSp(34f)`):
- ProbeCalibrate Focus detector/expand: `sizeDp = fsSp(56f, t.fs).dp` (the weighted square cells stay equal-width to the kept nozzle drawable; the glyph centers in its cell).
- ProbeIconButton glyphs: `sizeDp = fsSp(34f, t.fs).dp`.
- MoveScreen / ThemeEditor sites: the EXISTING `fsSp(20f)` / `fsSp(28f)` / `fsSp(40f)` magnitudes carried over verbatim.

This is a render-path consequence of the drawable→ligature swap (a drawable fills, a glyph is typographic), not a scope deviation. THEME-01 tints preserved at every site.
