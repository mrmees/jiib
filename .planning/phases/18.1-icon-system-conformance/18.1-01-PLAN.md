<objective>
Land all Wave-0 tooling and new assets that downstream flips/deletions depend on, WITHOUT
touching any existing call site or deleting any drawable. Four deliverables:

1. `tools/verify_ligatures.py` — a fonttools-based, device-free gate proving every needed Material
   Symbol name (existing ligatures + the 13 D-08 targets) resolves in the bundled font. Covers D-04.
   It is the load-bearing precondition for every drawable→ligature flip in later waves.
2. Record font provenance: VERIFY (do NOT swap) the bundled `material_symbols_outlined.ttf` v2.944 is
   a current variable cut with the FILL/wght/GRAD/opsz axes and that `verify_ligatures.py` prints
   `missing: []`. Satisfies SC-1 / D-01 as relaxed by D-13 ("verify the bundled font is a current
   variable cut that resolves every needed glyph, and record provenance") — the verification IS the
   deliverable, not a new binary.
3. Author the two OFFICIAL Google vector drawables `disabled_by_default.xml` / `warning.xml` (path
   verbatim from material-design-icons, white fill, +960 translate group) for the single classic-Views
   status-glyph site. They REPLACE the hand-traced status octagon/triangle and are KEEPERS (load-bearing
   for the Console row — D-05, D-07).
4. Add an additive `DinghyIconsTest` drift-guard method asserting that after the 18.1 flips the only
   `IconRef.Drawable` registry entries are the D-10 customs {Nozzle, HeatBed} (D-15 / SC-3b).

Purpose: nothing later can flip a drawable to a ligature, or delete a drawable, until (a) the ligature
gate proves the glyph renders and (b) the Views console row has official vectors to swap to.
Output: 1 script, 2 drawables, 1 additive test method, recorded font provenance.

NOTE: at the END of Wave 0 the drift-guard test will be RED (the registry flips are Plan 02) — that is
expected and correct (it is the RED half of a guard that goes GREEN in Wave 1). The
`verify_ligatures.py` gate and `:app:assembleDebug` MUST be green at the end of this plan.
