Purpose: D-02 P0 root-cause fix for wide recomposition; the field-type changes here cascade to consumer call sites in later waves (which is why this lands before the split + push-down).
Output: An `@Immutable` `PrinterState` tree with immutable collection fields and a reducer that emits them.
